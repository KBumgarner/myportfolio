About Me

I'm an educational content specialist with a decade of experience in assessment development.

I've had the privilege of working as a key contributor to the North Carolina End-of-Grade (EOG) Reading tests for grades 6, 7, and 8. I am passionate about creating innovative and equitable learning experiences.

My professional mission is to leverage technology and innovative practices to make education more accessible, engaging, and impactful for all learners.

Embodied Cognition Poster

View here

I conducted a pedagogical audit of the Saluda Historic Depot Museum through the lens of Embodied Cognition Theory. I developed an evaulative rubruic to measure how physical interactions and multisensory stimuli within the museum's exhibits impacted visitor engagment and knowledge construction. The project culminated in a strategic proposal and visual poster outlining actionable recommendations for transforming passive exhibits into active learning expieriences.

Formative Short-Essay Tutor

Designed the logic architecture and UX mockups for an AI-driven formative assessment tool focused on short-response writing. I developed a Natural Language Processing logic framework that enables the system to provide real-time, actionable feedback based on specific rubric critera. By mapping out the conditional feedback loops, I ensured the tutor could identify student misconceptions and guide them toward revision, mimicing the approach of a human writing coach.
