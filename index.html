About Me

I'm an educational content specialist with a decade of experience in assessment development.

I've had the privilege of working as a key contributor to the North Carolina End-of-Grade (EOG) Reading tests for grades 6, 7, and 8. I am passionate about creating innovative and equitable learning experiences.

My professional mission is to leverage technology and innovative practices to make education more accessible, engaging, and impactful for all learners.

Featured Projects

Selected work focused on instructional research and accessibility

Baby Steps Mobile App Prototype

Graduate Coursework · Team Project · Spring 2025

View Prototype

I led the market research phase to identify gaps in postpartum support, informing our team's user personas and initial ideation. I ensured all health content was accessible and empathetic while contributing to the iterative prototyping process in Figma to streamline the user journey.

Embodied Cognition Poster

Graduate Coursework · Solo Research Project · Fall 2024

View Poster

I conducted a learning-focused audit of the Saluda Historic Depot Museum using Embodied Cognition Theory. I created a rubric to measure how multisensory stimuli impacted visitor engagement. The project resulted in a strategic proposal to transform passive museum exhibits into active, hands-on learning experiences.

AIgnite Onboarding Website Prototype

Graduate Coursework · Team Project · Fall 2024

View Prototype

I collaborated on a Figma prototype for a web-based platform designed for onboarding new employees. I applied educational learning theories to the user flow to maximize knowledge retention and led user testing sessions. After analyzing user feedback, I helped simplify the user interface for users with low technical literacy.

Village of Wisdom (VOW) Market Analysis

Graduate Coursework · Team Project · Fall 2024

View Presentation

I collaborated with a team to perform a market analysis for VOW, an organization dedicated to providing black families with tools to advocate for culturally affirming learning environments. I co-developed a standardized evaluation rubric and authored the "Interactivity" metrics to assess user engagement. I refined the rubric criteria based on stakeholder feedback to ensure consistency across all final product evaluations.

Classroom Benchmark Item Bank (CBIB) Project Text Audit

Graduate Coursework · Internship · Fall 2025 - Spring 2026

View Presentation

I audited the CBIB database to align middle school ELA texts with new readability and word-count standards. I evaluated passages for grammar, copyright status, and grade-level appropriateness, while removing cultural and socioeconomic biases to ensure testing equity. Based on my findings, I made final determinations to retain, revise, or reject each text.
